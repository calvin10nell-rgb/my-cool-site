The hard truth

Why a $5,000 website is wasting your money.

Nobody wants to hear this from the people building websites. We'll say it anyway.

A marketing firm sells you a site. They show you mockups, you pick a colour, they launch it, they invoice you, and they walk away. Everyone shakes hands. Then nothing happens.

Because here's what a website actually does: it convinces a stranger to contact you. That's it. That's the whole job. The site did its part the second that person picked up the phone.

You were under a sink with both hands wet. The phone rang out. They called the next name on the list — and that name answered.

That call was the return on your $5,000. And it went to a competitor with a worse website and a better system.

A website without capture, nurture and trust attached to it is a brochure in a desert. Beautifully printed. Nobody around to read it. And the agency that sold it to you has no reason to care, because they already got paid.

We built our entire model around the opposite bet: the site is free, and we only matter if the phone turns into work.

What the pretty website didn't do 4 failures
It didn't answer at 6:40pm

Roughly 2 in 5 service calls come outside business hours. Nobody was there.

It didn't follow up on the missed call

No text, no callback, no record it ever happened. The lead evaporated silently.

It didn't talk to the 97% who just browsed

They read two paragraphs, didn't call, and closed the tab. Gone, unrecorded.

It didn't ask for a single review

Nine happy customers, zero stars added. Your competitor asked. That's the only difference.

The partnership model

We win when your business grows.

Free work makes people suspicious, and it should. So here's the whole arrangement, in plain terms.

The site costs us real hours. We absorb that, because it's the fastest way to show you what we're actually good at — instead of asking you to gamble a few thousand dollars on a proposal document.

You run it and judge it

Live site, no invoice, no obligation. If it brings you nothing, you owe us nothing and you keep the site. That's the risk we're carrying, and we're comfortable carrying it.
