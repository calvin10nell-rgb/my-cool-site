Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

In short: we collect the information you give us on the audit form, we use it to look at your Google Business Profile and follow up with you, and we don't sell it to anyone.

This page is a plain-language starting point, not a substitute for a lawyer's review — see the note at the bottom for what to check before this goes live.

Who this covers

This policy covers rocarev.com and the free audit application on it. It's written by RocaRev ("we," "us"). If you're a paying RocaRev customer, the terms of your service agreement govern how we handle your business's operational data (call logs, review requests, customer contacts) — this page is about the website itself.

Information we collect

When you fill out the audit form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your phone number
  • The Google review-count range you select

We don't ask for payment details, don't require an account, and don't collect anything beyond what's on that form. If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence like any other email.

How we use it

Solely to run the audit and follow up with you: we look up your Google Business Profile and the competitors ranking above you, then call or text the number you gave us to go over what we found. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't add you to a newsletter or drip sequence you didn't ask for.

Calls and texts

By submitting the form, you're agreeing that we can call or text the number you provided about your audit request. Standard message and data rates may apply if we text you. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time — reply STOP to a text, or just tell us on a call, and we'll honor it.

Who we share it with

Your form submission goes directly into our customer relationship management (CRM) system, which we use to track and respond to inquiries. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone outside of running our own business. If we ever change CRM providers, your information moves with us under the same terms.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't run ad trackers or analytics pixels. The only third-party request your browser makes is to Google Fonts, to load the typefaces used on this page — Google's standard privacy practices apply to that request. We don't set marketing or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep audit-request information for as long as it's useful for following up with you, and delete it on request. If you become a paying customer, your information is retained for the life of the business relationship plus whatever period we're required to keep records for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us what we have on file, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@rocarev.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and won't make you jump through hoops to do it.

Children

This site is for business owners and isn't directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you're okay with the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@rocarev.com.

Before this goes live: this draft is written to match what the current site actually does — it doesn't claim tracking or data use that isn't real, and it shouldn't be padded with boilerplate that isn't true either. Two things worth a lawyer's eye before launch: whether the SMS consent language above satisfies TCPA requirements for your state and how you're actually placing calls/texts, and whether you have customers in California, Colorado, or another state with its own privacy-disclosure law that adds requirements beyond what's here.
