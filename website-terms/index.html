Included Website Terms

Last updated: August 18, 2026

In short: the site is custom-built and free to build, it's yours, and hosting is on us for as long as you're a review-engine customer. If you ever cancel, you keep what we built.

1. What's included

A custom-built website — not a template with your logo swapped in — written to convert and optimized to load in under a second on mobile data. We handle hosting and SSL at no separate cost. There's no invoice for the build itself.

2. Timeline

We target seven days from when we have what we need from you (photos, business details, service area) to a live site. That's a target based on typical builds, not a guarantee — some builds move faster, some need more back-and-forth, and we'll tell you where things stand if a build is running long.

3. The domain name

We strongly recommend you own your domain name in your own registrar account (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, or similar) — that way it's never in question who controls it. If you'd like our help registering one, we're glad to walk you through it, but it goes in your name, not ours.

4. Hosting while you're a customer

As long as you're an active review-engine customer, we host the site, keep the SSL certificate current, and cover routine maintenance at no extra charge.

5. If you cancel

The site is yours. If you cancel the review-engine subscription, here's what happens to the website specifically:

  • We'll keep the site live for 30 days after cancellation, so you have time to move it or make other arrangements — no extra charge during that window.
  • Within that window, we'll give you an export of the site so you or another developer can host it elsewhere, or we'll help you move it to hosting of your choice.
  • After 30 days, if we haven't heard from you about where the site should go, we may take it offline — but the files remain yours on request even after that.

We won't hold the site hostage over a cancellation, and we won't quietly keep charging you for hosting you didn't agree to after you've left.

6. Revisions and support

Small content updates (text changes, photo swaps, hours or service-area updates) are included while you're an active customer. Larger changes — a redesign, new pages, new functionality — are scoped and quoted separately.

7. Your content

Photos, testimonials, logos, and business details you give us stay yours. We don't reuse your content for another client, and we don't stock-photo your site with someone else's business.

8. No traffic or conversion guarantee

The website is one part of the four-pillar system. On its own, a fast site doesn't guarantee a specific number of visitors, calls, or booked jobs — that's what the review engine, missed-call text-back, and chatbot are for. See our Terms of Service for the broader no-guarantee language that applies to the full service.

9. Changes to these terms

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about the website specifically: hello@rocarev.com.
